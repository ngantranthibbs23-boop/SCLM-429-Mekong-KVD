# 01. Industrial Park Comparison

## 1. Project Background

Mekong KVD planned to consolidate its four fragmented facilities into one centralized Mega Hub to improve logistics operations and distribution efficiency.

The project evaluated three candidate industrial parks to identify the most suitable location for the proposed Mega Hub.

## 2. Candidate Industrial Parks

The three candidate locations were:

- Binh Minh Industrial Park – Vinh Long
- Hoa Phu Industrial Park – Vinh Long
- Sa Dec Industrial Park (Zone C Expansion) – Dong Thap

## 3. Evaluation Criteria

The project used six criteria to compare the candidate locations:

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---:|
| CapEx Budget | 25% |
| Connectivity | 25% |
| Partners Accessibility | 20% |
| Remaining Lease | 15% |
| Infrastructure & Utilities | 10% |
| Expansion Potential | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

## 4. Weighted Scoring Model

Each industrial park was evaluated on a scale of 1 to 5.

| Criteria | Weight | Binh Minh | Hoa Phu | Sa Dec |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CapEx Budget | 25% | 4 | 3 | 5 |
| Connectivity | 25% | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| Partners Accessibility | 20% | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| Remaining Lease | 15% | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| Infrastructure & Utilities | 10% | 5 | 4 | 3 |
| Expansion Potential | 5% | 4 | 2 | 3 |
| **Total Score** | **100%** | **4.40** | **3.65** | **3.35** |

## 5. Final Recommendation

Binh Minh Industrial Park achieved the highest weighted score of 4.40 out of 5.

Its main advantages included:

- Strong transportation connectivity.
- Good accessibility to B2B partners.
- Suitable infrastructure and utilities.
- Potential for future expansion.

Therefore, Binh Minh Industrial Park was selected as the proposed location for Mekong KVD's Mega Hub.
